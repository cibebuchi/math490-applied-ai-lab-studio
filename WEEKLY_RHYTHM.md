# MATH 490 Weekly Rhythm

## Before class

**Instructor Setup — Prepare**

The instructor publishes the class question, dataset, starting variables, practical task, and required outputs.

## During class

**Today’s Lab — Learn**

Understand the concept and complete the readiness check.

**Practical Studio — Practise**

Run the class analysis. Starting variables load once; available controls remain freely selectable.

**Wrap-Up — Reflect**

Answer the module questions one at a time. Regular weeks contain 10 questions. Week 8 and Week 16 use 50-question comprehensive review sets. The scheduled class ends here.

## After class

**My Notebook — Apply independently**

Write your own research question, run your analysis, record evidence, and use the notebook to prepare your three-slide presentation.

## Available at any time

**Learning Library — Review**

Search key terms, study one selected model and its parameters, or review the Week 1–16 course map.

**Exam Practice — Prepare**

Complete focused or mixed sets of 5–20 questions and use the feedback to identify what to study next.
