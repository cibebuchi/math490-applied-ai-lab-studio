# Exam Practice Guide

Exam Practice is an independent revision area. It is not locked behind Practical Studio and does not replace the official Week 8 or Week 16 classroom reviews.

## Student workflow

1. Search for a curriculum topic.
2. Choose a topic.
3. Select Foundation, Application, and/or Calculation questions.
4. Choose 5, 10, 15, or 20 questions.
5. Answer one question at a time.
6. Check the answer and read the explanation.
7. Review the final score, weak concepts, and study advice.
8. Download the practice report when needed.

## Question sources

The practice pools combine:

- The reviewed weekly Wrap-Up questions
- The 50-question midterm review bank
- The 50-question final review bank
- Additional worked numerical questions

Every selected set contains unique question prompts and shuffles the answer positions.

## Score interpretation

- 90–100%: excellent command; move to mixed review or explain the answers aloud.
- 75–89%: strong progress; review missed concepts.
- 60–74%: developing understanding; study the glossary and redo calculations.
- Below 60%: revisit the weekly lesson and begin again with a shorter set.

Scores are diagnostic and are not automatically sent to a learning-management system.
